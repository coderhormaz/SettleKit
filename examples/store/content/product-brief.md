# Fieldwork · product brief workbook

This is the digital workbook supplied by the SettleKit demonstration store.
Test WBT and test USDT have no monetary value.

## The decision

What should someone be able to do after using this product?
Write one observable outcome, who needs it, and the moment it matters.

## Evidence before solutions

- Describe the current workaround and its cost.
- Record three concrete observations from real users.
- Separate confirmed facts, assumptions and unanswered questions.

## The smallest useful journey

Entry point → first action → decision → outcome → next step.
For each transition, describe loading, empty, error, unavailable and success states.
Include how someone recovers after leaving halfway through.

## Acceptance

1. Name the user-visible behavior.
2. Describe the input and the expected result.
3. Include one permission boundary and one failure/recovery example.
4. Decide how you will observe the result without collecting unnecessary data.

## Review

What could fail? Which assumptions have the largest cost if wrong?
Who owns the next decision, and what evidence will change it?
